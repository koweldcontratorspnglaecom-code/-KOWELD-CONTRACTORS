About Us

KOWELD CONTRACTORS is a professional engineering, welding, fabrication, and construction company based in Lae, Papua New Guinea.

Since our establishment on 26th July 2020, we have remained committed to delivering reliable, safe, and high-quality industrial solutions for clients across multiple industries.

Mission

To provide high-quality engineering, welding, and industrial services while building strong relationships based on trust, safety, integrity, and professional workmanship.

Vision

To become one of Papua New Guinea’s trusted industrial service providers by delivering innovative and reliable engineering solutions with the highest standards of quality and safety.

Services
